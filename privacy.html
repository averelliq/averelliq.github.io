AVERELLIQ - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the AVERELLIQ TikTok integration handles information when an authorized TikTok account is connected to our tools.

Information We May Process

Depending on the permissions granted through TikTok, the integration may process basic account information, authorization tokens, and information necessary to upload or publish authorized content.

How We Use Information

We use this information only to authenticate the connected account, provide requested publishing or content-management features, maintain service functionality, and protect the service from misuse.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by TikTok and other service providers only where necessary to operate the integration or comply with legal obligations.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service or meet legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards to protect stored credentials and access tokens.

Your Choices

You may revoke TikTok authorization through your TikTok account settings where available. You may also contact us to request deletion of information held directly by AVERELLIQ, subject to applicable legal requirements.
