## Voiceover (English)
"This line isn't ink. And your pencil doesn't contain lead. So what actually sticks to the page? Watch the tip. Paper is rough up close, and it scrapes tiny flakes of graphite off the pencil. Those flakes cling to the paper fibers. Here's the strange part: graphite is carbon, just like diamond, but its atoms are arranged differently. Its layers slide apart—that's how it writes. Your eraser lifts the graphite off again. A pencil mark is a trail of tiny carbon flakes."
